## 🛤️ From Security to Data

My starting point wasn't data pipelines — it was terminals, port scans, and figuring out how systems fail. Tools like **Nmap**, **Amass**, and **Hydra** taught me to think in terms of enumeration, attack surface, and automation long before I wrote my first DAG.

That background turns out to be useful in an unexpected way: Linux internals, networking, and scripting translate directly into orchestration, infrastructure, and the operational side of data engineering — the parts that don't show up in beginner tutorials.

**Data Engineering is where I'm putting my main effort.** Cybersecurity is the lens I bring to it, not a competing path.
